## Post 1 / 5

Introducing Orca 🐬

We've been watching Polymarket for a while and kept seeing the same thing. Prices that didn't add up.

---

## Post 2 / 5

"Trump wins Pennsylvania" priced higher than "Trump wins the election."

That's impossible. He has to win Pennsylvania to win the country. So one of those prices is wrong.

---

## Post 3 / 5

Buy the cheap one, sell the expensive one. You make money no matter what happens.

But these gaps open and close in seconds, all night long. Nobody can catch them by hand.

So we built the thing that does.

---

## Post 4 / 5

Orca is a bot that watches Polymarket for you.

You tell it what to do in one English sentence:

*"watch Trump markets, only take trades above 0.5% profit, max $50 per bet, cap $500."*

It finds the mispriced bets and sizes the trade.

---

## Post 5 / 5

Your wallet signs every trade. Your money never leaves your wallet. The bot only proposes. You stay in control.
